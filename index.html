Resilient Autonomy

Mission-driven planning, control, and learning for autonomous systems.

I develop the theoretical and computational foundations that allow autonomous systems to operate safely, cooperatively, and reliably in complex, uncertain, and dynamic environments. My group works from control theory and numerical optimization to experiments on aerial, underwater, ground, and soft robots.

Research program

An autonomous system is dependable only if the plan it computes, the controller that executes it, and the vehicles it coordinates with all hold up when conditions change. I treat these as one problem. Optimal control produces trajectories that respect dynamics and safety constraints; robust and adaptive control keeps the vehicle on them despite disturbances and model error; learning makes the computation fast enough to re-plan in real time; and cooperative control extends the guarantees from one vehicle to a team that shares the environment with people. Safety and resilience, meaning the ability to keep performing the mission when the environment, the communication network, or the vehicle itself changes, are the objectives that connect this work.

  • Optimal control and motion planning

    Direct methods based on Bernstein polynomials that make trajectory optimization tractable, with convergence guarantees and constraint checks over the entire trajectory.

  • Robust and adaptive control

    Controllers with guaranteed performance under uncertainty, disturbances, and changing dynamics, from underwater vehicles in waves to soft robots driven by artificial muscles.

  • Learning-enabled autonomy

    Transformer networks and robot learning built on Bernstein trajectories, so that learned planners run in real time while keeping constraints such as collision avoidance explicit.

  • Cooperative autonomous systems

    Trajectory generation, time coordination over time-varying networks, and collision avoidance for teams of vehicles with limited, intermittent communication.

  • Human-autonomy teaming

    Autonomous aircraft that share airspace with piloted aircraft near airports, pilot-centric trajectory optimization, and studies of how people interact with nearby drones.
