Venanzio Cichella

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Iowa, where I work on planning and control for teams of autonomous vehicles.

My research spans cooperative control, collision avoidance, optimal control, machine learning, and human-centered design of autonomous vehicles. I direct the Cooperative Autonomous Systems Lab, where my students and I take these methods from theory to experiments on real robots.

Background

  • 2024– Associate Professor of Mechanical Engineering, University of Iowa
  • 2025 Visiting Professor (sabbatical), University of Bologna, Italy
  • 2022– Faculty, Applied Mathematical and Computational Sciences, University of Iowa
  • 2020– Faculty Research Engineer, IIHR–Hydroscience & Engineering, University of Iowa
  • 2018–2024 Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering, University of Iowa
  • 2018 PhD in Mechanical Engineering, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Dissertation: “Planning and Control of Cooperative Autonomous Systems,” advised by Naira Hovakimyan
  • 2010–2012 Visiting Researcher, Naval Postgraduate School, Monterey, CA, with Isaac Kaminer and Vladimir Dobrokhodov
  • 2011 MS in Automation Engineering, University of Bologna, advised by Lorenzo Marconi and Isaac Kaminer, including an Erasmus exchange at TU Delft

Honors and awards

  • 2024 MechSE Outstanding Young Alumni Award, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
  • 2024 Innovation Challenge Phase I award for a RoboSoft simulator

Research interests

  • Optimal motion planning and numerical optimal control with Bernstein polynomials
  • Cooperative control and time coordination of multi-vehicle systems
  • Collision avoidance with safety guarantees
  • Safe, learning-enabled autonomy, including autonomous aircraft near airports
  • Robust and adaptive control of underwater vehicles
  • Modeling and control of soft robots and artificial muscles
