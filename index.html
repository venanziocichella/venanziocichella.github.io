Cooperative Autonomous Systems Lab

We develop and test algorithms for controlling multiple autonomous vehicles in real time.

The facility has a state-of-the-art motion capture system that provides position data with sub-millimeter accuracy. It also houses several quadrotor UAVs and ground robots. Students use the lab to design, implement, and test planning and control algorithms for autonomous systems.

Safety is a main concern. When vehicles are in use, they operate inside a large area closed off with a net. The lab is upgraded continuously and will house more robots for testing large swarms of autonomous vehicles.
