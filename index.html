Venanzio Cichella

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Iowa, where I work on planning and control for teams of autonomous vehicles.

My research spans cooperative control, collision avoidance, optimal control, machine learning, and human-centered design of autonomous vehicles. I direct the Cooperative Autonomous Systems Lab, where my students and I take these methods from theory to experiments on real robots.

Background

  • Now Associate Professor, Mechanical Engineering, University of Iowa
  • PhD Mechanical Engineering, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, advised by Naira Hovakimyan
  • Research Visiting Researcher, CAVR Lab, Naval Postgraduate School, Monterey, CA, with Isaac Kaminer and Vladimir Dobrokhodov
  • MS Automation Engineering, University of Bologna, advised by Lorenzo Marconi (including an Erasmus exchange at TU Delft)

Research interests

  • Cooperative planning and control of multi-vehicle systems
  • Collision avoidance with safety guarantees
  • Optimal motion planning and optimal control
  • Robust and adaptive control
  • Machine learning for autonomy, and human-centered autonomous vehicle design
