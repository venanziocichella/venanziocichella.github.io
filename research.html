Research

My research is on planning and control of autonomous systems, from single vehicles to cooperating teams. With my students in the CAS Lab, I work in four main areas.

Cooperative Autonomous Systems

We develop, implement, and test cooperative planning and control strategies for the safe execution of multi-vehicle missions in real-world environments.

Details

Robust and Adaptive Control

We design and test inner-loop adaptive controllers for complex maneuvers of underwater vehicles.

Details

Optimal Motion Planning

We develop and implement solutions to optimal motion planning problems for multi-vehicle missions.

Details

Control of Artificial Muscles

In collaboration with the SMMS Lab, we develop robotic arms driven by twisted and coiled artificial muscles.
