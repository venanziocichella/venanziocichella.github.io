Research

I develop the control and decision-making foundations that allow teams of autonomous robots to remain reliable when their models, information, and operating environments cannot be trusted.

On this page: Learning and adaptation with guarantees · Cooperative autonomy under uncertainty · Verified autonomy in the real world · Software · Sponsors

Resilient Cooperative Autonomy in Complex Environments

Thrust 1

Learning and adaptation with guarantees

Develop controllers that adapt to uncertain dynamics and changing operating conditions without sacrificing explicitly defined safety or stability properties: nonlinear control, adaptive control, robust control, and theoretical guarantees.

Thrust 2

Cooperative autonomy under uncertainty

Coordinate heterogeneous robots when models are imperfect, information is incomplete, communication is intermittent, and objectives change: optimal control, trajectory optimization, multi-vehicle planning, and cooperative systems.

Thrust 3

Verified autonomy in the real world

Connect theoretical guarantees to hardware demonstrations, human-robot interaction, and realistic field conditions: connecting mathematical methods to vehicles, experiments, and deployment.

Software

BeBOT (Bernstein/Bézier Optimal Trajectories) is my group’s open-source toolkit for generating optimal trajectories for one or many autonomous vehicles. It is available in Python, with MATLAB, C++, and CasADi versions. More code is on the lab GitHub.
